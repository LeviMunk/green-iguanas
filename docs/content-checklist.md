# Content and artwork needed for the first campaign content release

## Characters

The only facts published now are five roster names: Bobby Kills, Indigo, Owen, Freed, and Heilbrun.

For **each character**, send any of the following independently. Missing fields can remain blank:

- An approved transparent PNG/WebP or character illustration, ideally at least 1200 × 1600 pixels or equivalent. Keep all weapons and effects inside the edges. Provide a portrait crop if the full-body art doesn't suit mobile.
- Confirmed class/subclass, level, ancestry, background, player-visible biography and personality.
- Official shareable D&D Beyond URL (only if the character owner wants the URL displayed).
- Character-to-player ownership mapping for future private permissions (do **not** send passwords).
- Session milestones, items, relationships, and famous moments that are already player-known.
- Optional character-specific color accent or a revision request to the proposed accent palette.

The website supports approved art paths in `src/data/campaign.ts` but does not include invented fantasy portraits. Future DM uploads will require secure private storage and publishing tools.

## Campaign archive

- Verified session dates/numbers and short recap drafts.
- NPC names, faction associations, place names, and known relationships.
- Quests and whether they are active, complete, failed or unresolved.
- Dragonbowl bracket/opponents/matches/outcomes as confirmed by game records.
- Party treasury inventory and which details should be shared vs DM-only.
- Achievements the group has agreed are meaningful.

## Future TableOS integration

Do not supply database credentials or private tokens. When approved, share **redacted examples** of the existing dice events, stable character and session IDs, where the events are persisted, and export options. Reconcile the proposed contract against actual event behavior before running any exporter.

## Privacy decisions to make before inviting players

Decide whether character dossiers are public to the web, shared only with signed-in players, or private by default. Decide whether the DM may see player journal entries, and whether unpublished campaign clues should be hidden until actively published. The draft database defaults to members-only visibility, and truly private player journals remain owner-only unless explicitly shared.
